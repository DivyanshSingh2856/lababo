About

One farm in Uji, then a few more.

Our story

We started as one person's obsession.

Chashitsu Trading Co. began in 2021 after a trip to Uji, when our founder kept asking one small matcha farm to keep shipping a few tins a month long after the trip ended. A few friends asked to buy some too. Four years later it's the same handful of farms, just a lot more friends.

We still visit every farm we buy from. We still taste every batch before it goes on the site. And we're still a small team — which means when something breaks, we're usually the ones who notice first, not a customer.

How we work

Small enough to still do this by hand.

Every order is packed by two people in a single warehouse. Every tasting note on the site is written by someone who actually drank that batch. We like it that way, even if it means we grow slower.
